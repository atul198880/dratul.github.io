ABOUT

Academic Profile

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science with more than a decade of teaching and research experience. My academic interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, OCR and document image analysis.

My research work focuses particularly on the digitization and recognition of Indian-language documents, including Punjabi newspapers and Gurmukhi-script content.
